This guide covers **Delete selected duplicate copies** in Dosya. The main action is to choose Delete selected, read the count, and confirm; the decisive check is whether selected copies move to Deleted items while the keeper remains available.

## Before you start

- Open Duplicates in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify reviewed set of disposable duplicate copies.

## Steps: delete selected duplicate copies

1. Open Duplicates and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate reviewed set of disposable duplicate copies and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose Delete selected, read the count, and confirm.
4. Confirm that selected copies move to Deleted items while the keeper remains available.

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Selected copies move to Deleted items while the keeper remains available.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and selected copies move to Deleted items while the keeper remains available.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Duplicates, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
