This guide covers **Run a one-file upload preflight** in Dosya. The main action is to upload the sample to the final destination and inspect its name, size, preview, and access; the decisive check is whether the sample proves the destination and permissions are correct.

Track the test file with its destination, available storage and transfer state.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya upload view in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify one non-confidential sample from the planned source.

## Steps: run a one-file upload preflight

1. Open the Dosya upload view and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate one non-confidential sample from the planned source and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Upload the sample to the final destination and inspect its name, size, preview, and access.
4. Confirm that the sample proves the destination and permissions are correct.

**Safety note.** Confirm the workspace and destination before starting a large or important transfer.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The sample proves the destination and permissions are correct.

Compare the test file with its source by name, size and destination before removing a local copy. Record the exact transfer error for the test file before retrying; check size, quota and destination.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the sample proves the destination and permissions are correct.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya upload view, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
