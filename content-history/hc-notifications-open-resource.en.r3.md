This guide covers **Open the resource linked from a notification** in Dosya. The main action is to select the notification card and follow its in-app destination; the decisive check is whether dosya opens the related file, share, request, billing, or settings surface when available.

## Before you start

- Open Notifications in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify notification whose action is relevant.

## Steps: open the resource linked from a notification

1. Open Notifications and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate notification whose action is relevant and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Select the notification card and follow its in-app destination.
4. Confirm that Dosya opens the related file, share, request, billing, or settings surface when available.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Dosya opens the related file, share, request, billing, or settings surface when available.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and Dosya opens the related file, share, request, billing, or settings surface when available.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Notifications, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
