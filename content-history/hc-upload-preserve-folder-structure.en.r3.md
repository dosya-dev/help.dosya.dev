This guide covers **Preserve folder structure during an upload** in Dosya. The main action is to choose the folder upload action, review the detected tree, and start the controlled transfer; the decisive check is whether the nested folders appear under one destination without flattening.

Keep the source file, destination folder, available storage, and transfer state in view while you work.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya upload view in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify small local folder with two nested levels.

## Steps: preserve folder structure during an upload

1. Open the Dosya upload view and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate small local folder with two nested levels and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose the folder upload action, review the detected tree, and start the controlled transfer.
4. Confirm that the nested folders appear under one destination without flattening.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The nested folders appear under one destination without flattening.

Compare the uploaded item’s name, size, and destination with the source before removing any local copy. Record the failed item and its exact transfer message before retrying; repeated blind retries can hide a size, quota, or destination problem.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the nested folders appear under one destination without flattening.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya upload view, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
