This guide covers **Play video in the desktop app** in Dosya. The main action is to start playback, seek, adjust volume, and enter fullscreen once; the decisive check is whether the video responds to the controls without losing position.

## Before you start

- Open a media preview in the Dosya desktop app in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify supported video file.

## Steps: play video in the desktop app

1. Open a media preview in the Dosya desktop app and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate supported video file and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Start playback, seek, adjust volume, and enter fullscreen once.
4. Confirm that the video responds to the controls without losing position.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The video responds to the controls without losing position.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the video responds to the controls without losing position.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to a media preview in the Dosya desktop app, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
