This guide covers **Open the Dosya notification inbox** in Dosya. The main action is to open the inbox and let the newest page finish loading; the decisive check is whether notifications for the current account appear newest first.

## Before you start

- Open Notifications in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify notification bell or Notifications navigation item.

## Steps: open the Dosya notification inbox

1. Open Notifications and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate notification bell or Notifications navigation item and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open the inbox and let the newest page finish loading.
4. Confirm that notifications for the current account appear newest first.

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Notifications for the current account appear newest first.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and notifications for the current account appear newest first.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Notifications, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
