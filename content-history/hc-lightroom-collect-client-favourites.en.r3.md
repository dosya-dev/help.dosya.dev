This guide covers **Collect client favourites in a Lightroom gallery** in Dosya. The main action is to open the client link, enter a test client name, and mark a small set of photos; the decisive check is whether the gallery records that client and selection count.

## Before you start

- Open Lightroom Classic and the published Dosya gallery in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify gallery with favourites enabled.

## Steps: collect client favourites in a Lightroom gallery

1. Open Lightroom Classic and the published Dosya gallery and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate gallery with favourites enabled and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open the client link, enter a test client name, and mark a small set of photos.
4. Confirm that the gallery records that client and selection count.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The gallery records that client and selection count.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the gallery records that client and selection count.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Lightroom Classic and the published Dosya gallery, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
