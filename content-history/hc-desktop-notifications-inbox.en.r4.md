This guide covers **Review desktop notifications** in Dosya. The main action is to open it and inspect the newest items and destinations; the decisive check is whether notifications match the signed-in account and active context.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya desktop app and locate the notification bell in the desktop app.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify notification bell in the desktop app.

## Steps: review desktop notifications

1. Confirm the account and workspace in the Dosya desktop app when reviewing the notification bell in the desktop app.
2. Locate notification bell in the desktop app and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open it and inspect the newest items and destinations.
4. Confirm that notifications match the signed-in account and active context.

**Safety note.** Keyboard commands follow the focused window and may differ by operating system.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Notifications match the signed-in account and active context.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and notifications match the signed-in account and active context.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the notification bell in the desktop app, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
