This guide covers **Review access after changing a member role** in Dosya. The main action is to sign in as the test member or use a separate session to check navigation and one allowed and denied action; the decisive check is whether the new role takes effect across workspace controls.

## Before you start

- Open Workspace settings > Team in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify member whose role was just changed.

## Steps: review access after changing a member role

1. Open Workspace settings > Team and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate member whose role was just changed and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Sign in as the test member or use a separate session to check navigation and one allowed and denied action.
4. Confirm that the new role takes effect across workspace controls.

**Safety note.** Use a separate test member to verify access after every role or membership change.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The new role takes effect across workspace controls.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the new role takes effect across workspace controls.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Workspace settings > Team, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
