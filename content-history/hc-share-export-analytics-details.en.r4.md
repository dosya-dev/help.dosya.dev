## Before you start

- Open sharing-link Analytics and Activity in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify analytics export control and time range.

## Steps: export sharing analytics for review

1. Open sharing-link Analytics and Activity and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate analytics export control and time range and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose the required period, export, and open the file in a trusted tool.
4. Confirm that the export contains the visible event scope and expected columns.

**Safety note.** Analytics reflect recorded link events; privacy settings and client behavior can affect available detail.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The export contains the visible event scope and expected columns.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the export contains the visible event scope and expected columns.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to sharing-link Analytics and Activity, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
