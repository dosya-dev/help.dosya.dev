## Before you start

- Open sharing-link Analytics and Activity in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify access event outside the expected time, device, or recipient pattern.

## Steps: investigate unexpected sharing-link activity

1. Open sharing-link Analytics and Activity and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate access event outside the expected time, device, or recipient pattern and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Record the event, revoke or tighten the link, and contact the intended recipient through a separate channel.
4. Confirm that future access is contained while evidence remains available.

**Safety note.** Analytics reflect recorded link events; privacy settings and client behavior can affect available detail.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Future access is contained while evidence remains available.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and future access is contained while evidence remains available.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to sharing-link Analytics and Activity, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
