## Before you start

- Open a published collection in Lightroom Classic in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify gallery row on Integrations > Lightroom Classic.

## Steps: review Lightroom gallery status in Dosya

1. Open a published collection in Lightroom Classic and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate gallery row on Integrations > Lightroom Classic and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Compare title, item count, client count, selections, and link state.
4. Confirm that the web integration view matches the intended published collection.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The web integration view matches the intended published collection.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the web integration view matches the intended published collection.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to a published collection in Lightroom Classic, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
