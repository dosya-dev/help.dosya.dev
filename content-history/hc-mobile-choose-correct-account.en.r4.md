This guide covers **Choose the correct account on mobile** in Dosya. The main action is to select the intended email and sign-in method, then verify workspaces and profile after access; the decisive check is whether the mobile session belongs to the expected Dosya account.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya mobile app and device settings in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify mobile sign-in screen with one or more identity choices.

## Steps: choose the correct account on mobile

1. Open the Dosya mobile app and device settings and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate mobile sign-in screen with one or more identity choices and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Select the intended email and sign-in method, then verify workspaces and profile after access.
4. Confirm that the mobile session belongs to the expected Dosya account.

**Safety note.** Grant each operating-system permission only when its related feature is needed.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The mobile session belongs to the expected Dosya account.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the mobile session belongs to the expected Dosya account.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya mobile app and device settings, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
