- Open sharing-link Analytics and Activity in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify analytics summary for a live link.

## Steps: read sharing-link visitor counts

1. Open sharing-link Analytics and Activity and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate analytics summary for a live link and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Compare total visits, unique visitors, and the selected time window.
4. Confirm that the audience size is interpreted for the correct period.
