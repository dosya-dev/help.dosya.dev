## Before you start

- Open Account settings > Billing in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify known charge date or subscription period.

## Steps: locate an invoice for a billing period

1. Open Account settings > Billing and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate known charge date or subscription period and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open billing history, match the period and amount, and open the corresponding invoice entry.
4. Confirm that the document matches the intended Dosya charge and account.

**Safety note.** Match the account, billing period, amount, and plan before using a document for records.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The document matches the intended Dosya charge and account.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the document matches the intended Dosya charge and account.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Account settings > Billing, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
