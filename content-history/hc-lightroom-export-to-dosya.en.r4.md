This guide covers **Export photos from Lightroom directly to Dosya** in Dosya. The main action is to choose the destination folder, review rendering settings, and start Export; the decisive check is whether rendered photos appear in the chosen Dosya folder.

## Before you start

- Open Lightroom Classic Export in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Dosya service in the Export dialog.

## Steps: export photos from Lightroom directly to Dosya

1. Open Lightroom Classic Export and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate Dosya service in the Export dialog and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose the destination folder, review rendering settings, and start Export.
4. Confirm that rendered photos appear in the chosen Dosya folder.

**Safety note.** Use a small catalog sample and keep originals backed up independently while testing.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Rendered photos appear in the chosen Dosya folder.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and rendered photos appear in the chosen Dosya folder.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Lightroom Classic Export, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
