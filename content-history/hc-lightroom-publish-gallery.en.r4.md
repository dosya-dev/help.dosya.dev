This guide covers **Publish a Lightroom collection as a Dosya gallery** in Dosya. The main action is to choose Publish and keep Lightroom open while every rendition uploads; the decisive check is whether the collection reports published and the gallery appears in Dosya.

## Before you start

- Open Lightroom Classic Publish Services in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify published collection with photos waiting to publish.

## Steps: publish a Lightroom collection as a Dosya gallery

1. Open Lightroom Classic Publish Services and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate published collection with photos waiting to publish and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose Publish and keep Lightroom open while every rendition uploads.
4. Confirm that the collection reports published and the gallery appears in Dosya.

**Safety note.** Publish a small non-confidential collection first and leave Lightroom open until uploads finish.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The collection reports published and the gallery appears in Dosya.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the collection reports published and the gallery appears in Dosya.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Lightroom Classic Publish Services, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
