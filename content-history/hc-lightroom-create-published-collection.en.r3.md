This guide covers **Create a published Lightroom collection** in Dosya. The main action is to create a published collection, give it a client-facing name, and add test photos; the decisive check is whether the collection appears under the Dosya publish service with photos ready to publish.

## Before you start

- Open Lightroom Classic Publish Services in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify connected dosya.dev publish service.

## Steps: create a published Lightroom collection

1. Open Lightroom Classic Publish Services and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate connected dosya.dev publish service and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Create a published collection, give it a client-facing name, and add test photos.
4. Confirm that the collection appears under the Dosya publish service with photos ready to publish.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The collection appears under the Dosya publish service with photos ready to publish.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the collection appears under the Dosya publish service with photos ready to publish.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Lightroom Classic Publish Services, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
