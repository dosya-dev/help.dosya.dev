This guide covers **Reply to a file comment** in Dosya. The main action is to choose Reply, enter the response, and submit; the decisive check is whether the reply appears nested under the intended comment.

## Before you start

- Open a file preview and its Comments panel in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify comment that needs a contextual response.

## Steps: reply to a file comment

1. Open a file preview and its Comments panel and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate comment that needs a contextual response and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose Reply, enter the response, and submit.
4. Confirm that the reply appears nested under the intended comment.

**Safety note.** Use comments for file-specific context and avoid posting secrets or access credentials.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The reply appears nested under the intended comment.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the reply appears nested under the intended comment.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to a file preview and its Comments panel, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
