This guide covers **Replace a rejected file-request item** in Dosya. The main action is to read the exact message, prepare a compliant replacement, and retry that item; the decisive check is whether the replacement is accepted without resubmitting completed files.

## Before you start

- Open a Dosya file-request recipient page in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify item rejected for a stated type, size, or transfer reason.

## Steps: replace a rejected file-request item

1. Open a Dosya file-request recipient page and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate item rejected for a stated type, size, or transfer reason and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Read the exact message, prepare a compliant replacement, and retry that item.
4. Confirm that the replacement is accepted without resubmitting completed files.

**Safety note.** Uploaders should confirm the request owner and limits before selecting files.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The replacement is accepted without resubmitting completed files.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the replacement is accepted without resubmitting completed files.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to a Dosya file-request recipient page, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
