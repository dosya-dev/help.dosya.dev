- Open a file in the Dosya mobile app in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify supported ebook in the mobile library.

## Steps: read an ebook in the Dosya mobile app

1. Open a file in the Dosya mobile app and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate supported ebook in the mobile library and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open it, move between sections, and verify the current reading location.
4. Confirm that ebook navigation and text remain usable on the device.
