Use this workflow to read audio title and artist metadata in the intended workspace.

The player reads supported embedded tags; it does not infer or correct missing title, artist or album information.

## Before you start

- Open Files → Audio preview in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify a tagged audio file.

## Steps: read audio title and artist metadata

1. Open Files → Audio preview and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select a tagged audio file and check its name or identifying details.
3. Open the player and compare title, artist and album with the source file.
4. Verify that the player identifies the intended track.

**Safety note.** Use non-confidential test media and confirm the selected filename before playback.

## Verify the result

Compare the displayed fields with known source metadata and use the filename only as a secondary identifier.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and the player identifies the intended track.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Files → Audio preview, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
