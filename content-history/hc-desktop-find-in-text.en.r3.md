## Before you start

- Open the Dosya desktop app in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify open supported text preview.

## Steps: find text in a desktop file preview

1. Open the Dosya desktop app and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate open supported text preview and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open Find, enter the search text, and move between matches.
4. Confirm that each matching occurrence is highlighted in order.
