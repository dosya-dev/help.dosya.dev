## Steps: export sharing analytics safely

1. Open the intended sharing analytics view.
2. Set the required date range and filters.
3. Choose the supported export action.
4. Save the file to a controlled location.
5. Open and verify headers and row count.
6. Restrict access and delete temporary copies when finished.

Open the export, confirm its date range, headers and row count, then store it in a controlled location with access limited to the reviewers.
