This guide covers **Resolve a name conflict during restore** in Dosya. The main action is to compare both items, rename or relocate the existing one when appropriate, and retry the restore; the decisive check is whether both needed items remain distinguishable after restoration.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya Deleted items view in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify deleted item whose original name is already in use.

## Steps: resolve a name conflict during restore

1. Open the Dosya Deleted items view and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate deleted item whose original name is already in use and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Compare both items, rename or relocate the existing one when appropriate, and retry the restore.
4. Confirm that both needed items remain distinguishable after restoration.

**Safety note.** Restore first when uncertain; permanent deletion cannot be undone through Deleted items.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Both needed items remain distinguishable after restoration.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and both needed items remain distinguishable after restoration.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya Deleted items view, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
