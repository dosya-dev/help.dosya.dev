This guide covers **Clean up duplicates from the desktop app** in Dosya. The main action is to delete the selected copies and verify the keeper remains; the decisive check is whether only chosen copies move to Deleted items.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya desktop app and locate the reviewed disposable duplicate selection.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify reviewed disposable duplicate selection.

## Steps: clean up duplicates from the desktop app

1. Confirm the account and workspace in the Dosya desktop app when reviewing the reviewed disposable duplicate selection.
2. Locate reviewed disposable duplicate selection and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Delete the selected copies and verify the keeper remains.
4. Confirm that only chosen copies move to Deleted items.

**Safety note.** Confirm the active workspace and test destructive duplicate cleanup with disposable copies.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Only chosen copies move to Deleted items.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and only chosen copies move to Deleted items.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the reviewed disposable duplicate selection, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
