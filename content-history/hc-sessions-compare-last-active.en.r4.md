This guide covers **Compare session last-active times** in Dosya. The main action is to refresh the page, perform one harmless action on the current device, and compare the updated time and client details; the decisive check is whether the recently used session can be identified without guessing from location alone.

## Before you start

- Open Account settings > Security > Active sessions in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify two similar session entries.

## Steps: compare session last-active times

1. Open Account settings > Security > Active sessions and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate two similar session entries and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Refresh the page, perform one harmless action on the current device, and compare the updated time and client details.
4. Confirm that the recently used session can be identified without guessing from location alone.

**Safety note.** Compare device, browser, time, and location context before revoking a session.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The recently used session can be identified without guessing from location alone.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the recently used session can be identified without guessing from location alone.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Account settings > Security > Active sessions, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
