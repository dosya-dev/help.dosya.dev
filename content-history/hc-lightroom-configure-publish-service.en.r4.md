This guide covers **Configure the Dosya Lightroom publish service** in Dosya. The main action is to open its settings, review JPEG, size, color, metadata, and destination defaults, then save; the decisive check is whether the service displays the saved publish settings.

## Before you start

- Open Lightroom Classic Publish Services in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify dosya.dev service in Publish Services.

## Steps: configure the Dosya Lightroom publish service

1. Open Lightroom Classic Publish Services and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate dosya.dev service in Publish Services and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open its settings, review JPEG, size, color, metadata, and destination defaults, then save.
4. Confirm that the service displays the saved publish settings.

**Safety note.** Publish a small non-confidential collection first and leave Lightroom open until uploads finish.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The service displays the saved publish settings.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the service displays the saved publish settings.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Lightroom Classic Publish Services, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
