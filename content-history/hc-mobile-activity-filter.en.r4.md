This guide covers **Filter workspace activity on mobile** in Dosya. The main action is to choose a category, member, or action and apply the selection; the decisive check is whether the mobile activity list narrows to matching events.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya mobile app and locate the filter control on the Activity screen.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify filter control on the Activity screen.

## Steps: filter workspace activity on mobile

1. Confirm the account and workspace in the Dosya mobile app when reviewing the filter control on the Activity screen.
2. Locate filter control on the Activity screen and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose a category, member, or action and apply the selection.
4. Confirm that the mobile activity list narrows to matching events.

**Safety note.** Keep the mobile app updated and allow notifications at both the app and operating-system level.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The mobile activity list narrows to matching events.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the mobile activity list narrows to matching events.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the filter control on the Activity screen, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
