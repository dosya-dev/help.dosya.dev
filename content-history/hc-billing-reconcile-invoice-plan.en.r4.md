This guide covers **Reconcile an invoice with the active plan** in Dosya. The main action is to compare plan name, billing interval, included storage, adjustments, tax, and final total; the decisive check is whether the invoice line items explain the charge for the active billing period.

## Before you start

- Open Account settings > Billing in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify invoice and current plan summary.

## Steps: reconcile an invoice with the active plan

1. Open Account settings > Billing and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate invoice and current plan summary and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Compare plan name, billing interval, included storage, adjustments, tax, and final total.
4. Confirm that the invoice line items explain the charge for the active billing period.

**Safety note.** Match the account, billing period, amount, and plan before using a document for records.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The invoice line items explain the charge for the active billing period.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the invoice line items explain the charge for the active billing period.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Account settings > Billing, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
