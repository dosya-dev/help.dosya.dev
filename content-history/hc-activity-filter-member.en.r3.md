## Before you start

- Open Activity log in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Member filter above the activity list.

## Steps: filter activity by workspace member

1. Open Activity log and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate Member filter above the activity list and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Select the member whose actions you are investigating.
4. Confirm that the list contains permitted events attributed to that member.
