## Before you start

- Open the Dosya mobile app and locate the reviewed non-executable entry inside an archive.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify reviewed non-executable entry inside an archive.

## Steps: save one archive entry on mobile

1. Confirm the account and workspace in the Dosya mobile app when reviewing the reviewed non-executable entry inside an archive.
2. Locate reviewed non-executable entry inside an archive and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose the entry, select a safe destination, and save it.
4. Confirm that only the selected entry is written to the destination.
