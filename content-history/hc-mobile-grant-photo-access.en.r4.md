This guide covers **Grant photo access for mobile backup** in Dosya. The main action is to choose the smallest permission scope that contains the intended media, then return to Dosya and refresh selection; the decisive check is whether only permitted photos and videos are available to the backup workflow.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya mobile app and device settings in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify photo-backup setup and operating-system permission prompt.

## Steps: grant photo access for mobile backup

1. Open the Dosya mobile app and device settings and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate photo-backup setup and operating-system permission prompt and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose the smallest permission scope that contains the intended media, then return to Dosya and refresh selection.
4. Confirm that only permitted photos and videos are available to the backup workflow.

**Safety note.** Grant each operating-system permission only when its related feature is needed.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Only permitted photos and videos are available to the backup workflow.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and only permitted photos and videos are available to the backup workflow.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya mobile app and device settings, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
