Use this workflow to back up contacts from your phone in the intended account or workspace.

Check mobile account, workspace and device permissions for a small test contact set. Try a small test contact set with non-confidential data and verify its cloud copy in Files before removing device data.

## Before you start

- Open Mobile Contacts backup in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify a small test contact set.

## Steps: back up contacts from your phone

1. Open Mobile Contacts backup and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select a small test contact set and check its name or identifying details.
3. Grant contacts access, choose the source and start backup.
4. Verify that a contact backup completes with the expected count.

**Safety note.** Use a small test contact set and confirm the device contact account before a restore.

## Verify the result

Verify that a contact backup completes with the expected count.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and a contact backup completes with the expected count.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Mobile Contacts backup, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
