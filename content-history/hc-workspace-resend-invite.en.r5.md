Use this workflow to resend a workspace invitation in the intended account or workspace.

Resending should reuse the intended pending invitation rather than create a second membership path. Check the recipient address and role first.

## Before you start

- Open Workspace → Members in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify one pending test invitation.

## Steps: resend a workspace invitation

1. Open Workspace → Members and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select one pending test invitation and check its name or identifying details.
3. Choose Resend and confirm the recipient address.
4. Verify that the invitation shows a new sent time.

**Safety note.** Select the intended workspace and verify your administrator role before changing membership.

## Verify the result

Refresh the member list and confirm the pending row shows a new sent time for the same email and role.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and the invitation shows a new sent time.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Workspace → Members, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
