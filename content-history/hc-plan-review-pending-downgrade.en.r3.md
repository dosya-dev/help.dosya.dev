This guide covers **Review a pending plan downgrade** in Dosya. The main action is to confirm the target tier, effective date, current usage, and any action required before renewal; the decisive check is whether the account can reach the target limit before the downgrade takes effect.

## Before you start

- Open Account settings > Plan and storage in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify plan page showing a future downgrade.

## Steps: review a pending plan downgrade

1. Open Account settings > Plan and storage and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate plan page showing a future downgrade and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Confirm the target tier, effective date, current usage, and any action required before renewal.
4. Confirm that the account can reach the target limit before the downgrade takes effect.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The account can reach the target limit before the downgrade takes effect.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the account can reach the target limit before the downgrade takes effect.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Account settings > Plan and storage, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
