This guide covers **Remove a photo from a Lightroom gallery** in Dosya. The main action is to remove it from the collection and publish the pending removal; the decisive check is whether the photo leaves the gallery while its Dosya file remains unless trash-on-remove is enabled.

## Before you start

- Open a published collection in Lightroom Classic in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify photo currently in a published collection.

## Steps: remove a photo from a Lightroom gallery

1. Open a published collection in Lightroom Classic and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate photo currently in a published collection and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Remove it from the collection and publish the pending removal.
4. Confirm that the photo leaves the gallery while its Dosya file remains unless trash-on-remove is enabled.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The photo leaves the gallery while its Dosya file remains unless trash-on-remove is enabled.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the photo leaves the gallery while its Dosya file remains unless trash-on-remove is enabled.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to a published collection in Lightroom Classic, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
