This guide covers **Preflight a batch move** in Dosya. The main action is to check every selected item, confirm the destination workspace and breadcrumb, and move a disposable sample group first; the decisive check is whether the selected group appears once in the intended destination.

## Before you start

- Open a Dosya file or search-results list in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify reviewed selection and destination folder.

## Steps: preflight a batch move

1. Open a Dosya file or search-results list and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate reviewed selection and destination folder and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Check every selected item, confirm the destination workspace and breadcrumb, and move a disposable sample group first.
4. Confirm that the selected group appears once in the intended destination.

**Safety note.** Review the selection count and every selected row before starting a batch action.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The selected group appears once in the intended destination.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the selected group appears once in the intended destination.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to a Dosya file or search-results list, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
