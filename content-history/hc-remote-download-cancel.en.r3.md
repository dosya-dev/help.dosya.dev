This guide covers **Cancel an active remote download** in Dosya. The main action is to choose its X control and confirm the action if prompted; the decisive check is whether the transfer stops and the job becomes cancelled or leaves the list.

## Before you start

- Open Integrations > Remote Downloads in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify queued or active disposable job.

## Steps: cancel an active remote download

1. Open Integrations > Remote Downloads and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate queued or active disposable job and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose its X control and confirm the action if prompted.
4. Confirm that the transfer stops and the job becomes cancelled or leaves the list.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The transfer stops and the job becomes cancelled or leaves the list.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the transfer stops and the job becomes cancelled or leaves the list.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Integrations > Remote Downloads, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
