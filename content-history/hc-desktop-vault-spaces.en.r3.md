This guide covers **Browse Vault Spaces on desktop** in Dosya. The main action is to open a Space and move through its folder tree; the decisive check is whether only Spaces available to the account are shown.

## Before you start

- Open Vault in the Dosya desktop app in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify unlocked Vault navigation.

## Steps: browse Vault Spaces on desktop

1. Open Vault in the Dosya desktop app and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate unlocked Vault navigation and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open a Space and move through its folder tree.
4. Confirm that only Spaces available to the account are shown.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Only Spaces available to the account are shown.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and only Spaces available to the account are shown.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Vault in the Dosya desktop app, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
