This guide covers **Review connected Lightroom catalogs** in Dosya. The main action is to compare each computer name, connection date, and last-used time with your active catalogs; the decisive check is whether every expected catalog has one recognizable connection.

## Before you start

- Open Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Your Lightroom connections table.

## Steps: review connected Lightroom catalogs

1. Open Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate Your Lightroom connections table and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Compare each computer name, connection date, and last-used time with your active catalogs.
4. Confirm that every expected catalog has one recognizable connection.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Every expected catalog has one recognizable connection.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and every expected catalog has one recognizable connection.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
