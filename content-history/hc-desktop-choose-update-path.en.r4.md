This guide covers **Choose the correct desktop update path** in Dosya. The main action is to identify the installation source, use its supported updater, and verify the publisher before applying the update; the decisive check is whether the app updates through the channel that owns the installation.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya desktop app and its settings in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify desktop installation from a known store or direct installer.

## Steps: choose the correct desktop update path

1. Open the Dosya desktop app and its settings and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate desktop installation from a known store or direct installer and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Identify the installation source, use its supported updater, and verify the publisher before applying the update.
4. Confirm that the app updates through the channel that owns the installation.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The app updates through the channel that owns the installation.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the app updates through the channel that owns the installation.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya desktop app and its settings, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
