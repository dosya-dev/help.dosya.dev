This guide covers **Revoke a Lightroom catalog connection** in Dosya. The main action is to open its actions menu, choose Revoke, and confirm the warning; the decisive check is whether the connection disappears while previously published galleries remain online.

## Before you start

- Open Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic and locate the connection for the catalog that should be signed out.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify connection for the catalog that should be signed out.

## Steps: revoke a Lightroom catalog connection

1. Confirm the account and workspace in Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic when reviewing the connection for the catalog that should be signed out.
2. Locate connection for the catalog that should be signed out and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open its actions menu, choose Revoke, and confirm the warning.
4. Confirm that the connection disappears while previously published galleries remain online.

**Safety note.** Use Lightroom Classic 10 or newer and keep the plug-in folder in a permanent local location.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The connection disappears while previously published galleries remain online.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the connection disappears while previously published galleries remain online.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the connection for the catalog that should be signed out, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
