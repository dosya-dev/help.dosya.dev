## Before you start

- Open a WebDAV client connected to Dosya and locate the Finder Connect to Server dialog.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Finder Connect to Server dialog.

## Steps: mount Dosya WebDAV in macOS Finder

1. Confirm the account and workspace in a WebDAV client connected to Dosya when reviewing the Finder Connect to Server dialog.
2. Locate Finder Connect to Server dialog and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Enter the documented HTTPS WebDAV address and restricted credentials.
4. Confirm that Finder mounts the permitted Dosya location.

The controlled workflow completes and Finder mounts the permitted Dosya location.
