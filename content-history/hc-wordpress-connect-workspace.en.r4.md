This guide covers **Connect a WordPress site to a Dosya workspace** in Dosya. The main action is to open the approval page, sign in, choose a workspace, and allow the site; the decisive check is whether the site appears in Your WordPress sites.

## Before you start

- Open WordPress wp-admin and Integrations > WordPress and locate the Connect to dosya control in wp-admin.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Connect to dosya control in wp-admin.

## Steps: connect a WordPress site to a Dosya workspace

1. Confirm the account and workspace in WordPress wp-admin and Integrations > WordPress when reviewing the Connect to dosya control in wp-admin.
2. Locate Connect to dosya control in wp-admin and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open the approval page, sign in, choose a workspace, and allow the site.
4. Confirm that the site appears in Your WordPress sites.

**Safety note.** Install the plug-in only from the official Dosya download and connect it through wp-admin.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The site appears in Your WordPress sites.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the site appears in Your WordPress sites.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the Connect to dosya control in wp-admin, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
