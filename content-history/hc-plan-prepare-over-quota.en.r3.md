This guide covers **Prepare an over-quota account for a plan change** in Dosya. The main action is to identify disposable data and retained versions, download any required copy, reduce usage safely, and recheck the total; the decisive check is whether current usage is below the future allowance with room for normal operations.

## Before you start

- Open Account settings > Plan and storage in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify usage above the planned storage allowance.

## Steps: prepare an over-quota account for a plan change

1. Open Account settings > Plan and storage and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate usage above the planned storage allowance and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Identify disposable data and retained versions, download any required copy, reduce usage safely, and recheck the total.
4. Confirm that current usage is below the future allowance with room for normal operations.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Current usage is below the future allowance with room for normal operations.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and current usage is below the future allowance with room for normal operations.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Account settings > Plan and storage, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
