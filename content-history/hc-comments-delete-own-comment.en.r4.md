## Before you start

- Open a file preview and its Comments panel in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify disposable comment authored by your account.

## Steps: delete your own file comment

1. Open a file preview and its Comments panel and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate disposable comment authored by your account and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open its author controls, choose Delete, and confirm.
4. Confirm that the comment is removed from the thread without deleting the file.
