This guide covers **Review billing identity on an invoice** in Dosya. The main action is to check the account or company name, billing address, tax fields, currency, and subscription period against current records; the decisive check is whether the invoice identity is correct or the fields needing an update are known.

## Before you start

- Open Account settings > Billing in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify invoice preview or downloaded invoice.

## Steps: review billing identity on an invoice

1. Open Account settings > Billing and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate invoice preview or downloaded invoice and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Check the account or company name, billing address, tax fields, currency, and subscription period against current records.
4. Confirm that the invoice identity is correct or the fields needing an update are known.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The invoice identity is correct or the fields needing an update are known.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the invoice identity is correct or the fields needing an update are known.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Account settings > Billing, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
