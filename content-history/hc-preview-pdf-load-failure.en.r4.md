This guide covers **Troubleshoot a PDF preview** in Dosya. The main action is to verify upload completion, test a known page, and download the original to a trusted PDF reader; the decisive check is whether the issue is isolated to preview processing or to the source PDF.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya file viewer in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify PDF that opens incompletely or reports a preview error.

## Steps: troubleshoot a PDF preview

1. Open the Dosya file viewer and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate PDF that opens incompletely or reports a preview error and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Verify upload completion, test a known page, and download the original to a trusted PDF reader.
4. Confirm that the issue is isolated to preview processing or to the source PDF.

**Safety note.** A preview problem does not by itself mean the stored original is damaged.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The issue is isolated to preview processing or to the source PDF.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the issue is isolated to preview processing or to the source PDF.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya file viewer, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
