This guide covers **Verify storage after cleanup** in Dosya. The main action is to record usage, delete the items permanently, wait for accounting, and compare the updated breakdown; the decisive check is whether used storage decreases by the accounted data after processing.

## Before you start

- Open the workspace storage and usage view in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify known disposable files selected for permanent deletion.

## Steps: verify storage after cleanup

1. Open the workspace storage and usage view and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate known disposable files selected for permanent deletion and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Record usage, delete the items permanently, wait for accounting, and compare the updated breakdown.
4. Confirm that used storage decreases by the accounted data after processing.

**Safety note.** Allow storage accounting to update after transfers or permanent deletion before comparing totals.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Used storage decreases by the accounted data after processing.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and used storage decreases by the accounted data after processing.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the workspace storage and usage view, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
