This guide covers **Open photos on the Dosya map** in Dosya. The main action is to open Map and let its clustered photo markers load; the decisive check is whether geotagged files appear in their recorded locations.

## Before you start

- Open Map in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Map navigation item in the active workspace.

## Steps: open photos on the Dosya map

1. Open Map and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate Map navigation item in the active workspace and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open Map and let its clustered photo markers load.
4. Confirm that geotagged files appear in their recorded locations.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Geotagged files appear in their recorded locations.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and geotagged files appear in their recorded locations.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Map, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
