## Before you start

- Open a media file in the Dosya mobile viewer in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify multi-page PDF.

## Steps: read a PDF on mobile

1. Open a media file in the Dosya mobile viewer and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate multi-page PDF and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open it, zoom text, and move to a known page.
4. Confirm that the expected page renders legibly.

## Troubleshooting
