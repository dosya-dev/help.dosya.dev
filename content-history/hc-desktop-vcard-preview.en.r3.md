## Before you start

- Open the Dosya desktop app in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify VCF file in the desktop library.

## Steps: preview a vCard in the desktop app

1. Open the Dosya desktop app and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate VCF file in the desktop library and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open it and review the parsed contact fields.
4. Confirm that the contact data is readable before use elsewhere.
