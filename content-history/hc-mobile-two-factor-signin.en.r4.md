Use this workflow to complete two-factor sign-in on mobile in the intended account or workspace.

Two-factor sign-in requires the account password and a current verification code. Codes expire quickly and should never be shared.

## Before you start

- Open Mobile Sign in in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify an account with two-factor authentication.

## Steps: complete two-factor sign-in on mobile

1. Open Mobile Sign in and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select an account with two-factor authentication and check its name or identifying details.
3. Enter the password, then the current verification code.
4. Verify that the session opens only after both factors succeed.

## Verify the result

Confirm the session opens only after both checks, then review the new mobile session from account security settings.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and the session opens only after both factors succeed.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Mobile Sign in, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
