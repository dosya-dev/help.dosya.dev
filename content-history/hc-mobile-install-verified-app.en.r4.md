This guide covers **Install a verified Dosya mobile app** in Dosya. The main action is to confirm the publisher, app name, supported operating system, and store account before installing; the decisive check is whether the installed app opens to the expected Dosya sign-in screen.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya mobile app and device settings in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify official app-store listing for the device.

## Steps: install a verified Dosya mobile app

1. Open the Dosya mobile app and device settings and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate official app-store listing for the device and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Confirm the publisher, app name, supported operating system, and store account before installing.
4. Confirm that the installed app opens to the expected Dosya sign-in screen.

**Safety note.** Grant each operating-system permission only when its related feature is needed.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The installed app opens to the expected Dosya sign-in screen.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the installed app opens to the expected Dosya sign-in screen.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya mobile app and device settings, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
