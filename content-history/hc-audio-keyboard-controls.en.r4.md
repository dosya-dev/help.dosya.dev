This guide covers **Use keyboard controls for audio playback** in Dosya. The main action is to use the supported play, pause, seek, or volume keys and watch the player state; the decisive check is whether the player responds once per key without triggering page actions.

## Before you start

- Open an audio file in the Dosya player in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify focused Dosya audio player.

## Steps: use keyboard controls for audio playback

1. Open an audio file in the Dosya player and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate focused Dosya audio player and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Use the supported play, pause, seek, or volume keys and watch the player state.
4. Confirm that the player responds once per key without triggering page actions.

**Safety note.** Use non-sensitive media and lower device volume before testing playback controls.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The player responds once per key without triggering page actions.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the player responds once per key without triggering page actions.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to an audio file in the Dosya player, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
