This guide covers **Respond when a member upload reaches quota** in Dosya. The main action is to confirm the active workspace, review usage and allocation as an owner, then free or add capacity before retrying; the decisive check is whether the member can upload only after valid capacity is available.

## Before you start

- Open the workspace storage and usage view in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify member upload rejected for workspace storage.

## Steps: respond when a member upload reaches quota

1. Open the workspace storage and usage view and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate member upload rejected for workspace storage and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Confirm the active workspace, review usage and allocation as an owner, then free or add capacity before retrying.
4. Confirm that the member can upload only after valid capacity is available.

**Safety note.** Allow storage accounting to update after transfers or permanent deletion before comparing totals.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The member can upload only after valid capacity is available.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the member can upload only after valid capacity is available.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the workspace storage and usage view, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
