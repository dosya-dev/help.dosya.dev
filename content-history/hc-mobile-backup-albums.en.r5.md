Use this workflow to choose albums for mobile backup in the intended workspace.

Album selection narrows which accessible library items are eligible for future backup. Limited photo permission can narrow the effective set further.

## Before you start

- Open Mobile Backup settings in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify one small photo album.

## Steps: choose albums for mobile backup

1. Open Mobile Backup settings and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select one small photo album and check its name or identifying details.
3. Enable the intended album and leave unrelated albums disabled.
4. Verify that coverage begins only for the selected source.

**Safety note.** Keep the app updated and review operating-system photo and background permissions.

## Verify the result

Add one harmless photo to a selected album and confirm that it becomes eligible while an unselected album remains outside the scope.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and coverage begins only for the selected source.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Mobile Backup settings, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
