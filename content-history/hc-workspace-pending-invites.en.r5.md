Use this workflow to review pending workspace invitations in the intended account or workspace.

Pending invitations retain the email, proposed role and sent time until accepted, revoked or expired. Review stale rows before resending.

## Before you start

- Open Workspace → Members in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify the pending invitation list.

## Steps: review pending workspace invitations

1. Open Workspace → Members and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select the pending invitation list and check its name or identifying details.
3. Check email, role and sent time for each pending invite.
4. Verify that stale or incorrect invitations are identified.

**Safety note.** Select the intended workspace and verify your administrator role before changing membership.

## Verify the result

Reload the member list and identify each invitation that is correct, stale or addressed to the wrong person before changing it.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and stale or incorrect invitations are identified.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Workspace → Members, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
