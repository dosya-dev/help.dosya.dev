Use this workflow to restore contacts to a phone in the intended account or workspace.

Check mobile account, workspace and device permissions for a test contact backup. Try a test contact backup with non-confidential data and verify its cloud copy in Files before removing device data.

## Before you start

- Open Mobile Contacts restore in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify a test contact backup.

## Steps: restore contacts to a phone

1. Open Mobile Contacts restore and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select a test contact backup and check its name or identifying details.
3. Review the backup date and restore to the intended account.
4. Verify that the restored contacts appear in the device contacts app.

**Safety note.** Use a small test contact set and confirm the device contact account before a restore.

## Verify the result

Verify that the restored contacts appear in the device contacts app.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and the restored contacts appear in the device contacts app.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Mobile Contacts restore, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
