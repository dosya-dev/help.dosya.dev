This guide covers **Browse an archive on mobile** in Dosya. The main action is to open its archive view and expand folders without extracting everything; the decisive check is whether archive entries and paths are visible.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya mobile app and locate the supported ZIP or archive file.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify supported ZIP or archive file.

## Steps: browse an archive on mobile

1. Confirm the account and workspace in the Dosya mobile app when reviewing the supported ZIP or archive file.
2. Locate supported ZIP or archive file and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open its archive view and expand folders without extracting everything.
4. Confirm that archive entries and paths are visible.

**Safety note.** Confirm the active workspace and selected file before using mobile actions.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Archive entries and paths are visible.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and archive entries and paths are visible.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the supported ZIP or archive file, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
