This guide covers **Grant sharing permissions in a custom role** in Dosya. The main action is to enable the exact create, manage, or analytics capabilities needed; the decisive check is whether a test member sees only the intended sharing controls.

## Before you start

- Open Workspace settings > Roles in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify sharing permissions in the role editor.

## Steps: grant sharing permissions in a custom role

1. Open Workspace settings > Roles and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate sharing permissions in the role editor and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Enable the exact create, manage, or analytics capabilities needed.
4. Confirm that a test member sees only the intended sharing controls.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: A test member sees only the intended sharing controls.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and a test member sees only the intended sharing controls.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Workspace settings > Roles, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
