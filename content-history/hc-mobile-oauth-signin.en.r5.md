Use this workflow to sign in on mobile with Google, Apple or GitHub in the intended account or workspace.

Check mobile account, workspace and device permissions for the intended identity provider. Try the intended identity provider with non-confidential data and verify its cloud copy in Files before removing device data.

## Before you start

- Open Mobile Sign in in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify the intended identity provider.

## Steps: sign in on mobile with Google, Apple or GitHub

1. Open Mobile Sign in and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select the intended identity provider and check its name or identifying details.
3. Start the provider flow and confirm the account shown before returning.
4. Verify that Dosya opens the account linked to that provider.

**Safety note.** Confirm the signed-in account before changing device or sign-in security.

## Verify the result

Verify that Dosya opens the account linked to that provider.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and Dosya opens the account linked to that provider.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Mobile Sign in, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
