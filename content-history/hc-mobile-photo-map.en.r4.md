## Before you start

- Open the Dosya mobile app and locate the Map screen in the mobile app.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Map screen in the mobile app.

## Steps: explore geotagged photos on the mobile map

1. Confirm the account and workspace in the Dosya mobile app when reviewing the Map screen in the mobile app.
2. Locate Map screen in the mobile app and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open the map, expand a cluster, and select a known marker.
4. Confirm that the matching file preview opens from its location.
