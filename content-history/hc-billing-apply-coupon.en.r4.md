Use this workflow to apply a promotion or coupon in the intended account or workspace.

A valid coupon should update the displayed checkout total before payment. Its eligibility, duration and interval can limit the discount.

## Before you start

- Open Billing checkout in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify a valid promotion code.

## Steps: apply a promotion or coupon

1. Open Billing checkout and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select a valid promotion code and check its name or identifying details.
3. Enter the code before payment and review the updated total.
4. Verify that the discount and billing interval are visible before confirmation.

## Verify the result

Confirm the code, discount, billing interval and final total in the checkout summary before completing the purchase.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and the discount and billing interval are visible before confirmation.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Billing checkout, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
