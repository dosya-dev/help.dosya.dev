Use this workflow to switch accounts in the mobile app in the intended account or workspace.

Each mobile account has separate workspace context, files and backup settings. Check the selected identity before browsing or enabling backup.

## Before you start

- Open Mobile Profile in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify two authorized test accounts.

## Steps: switch accounts in the mobile app

1. Open Mobile Profile and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select two authorized test accounts and check its name or identifying details.
3. Add or choose the second account and verify its workspace.
4. Verify that files and backup settings belong to the selected account.

**Safety note.** Confirm the signed-in account before changing device or sign-in security.

## Verify the result

Open a known folder and review Backup settings after switching, confirming both belong to the intended account and workspace.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and files and backup settings belong to the selected account.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Mobile Profile, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
