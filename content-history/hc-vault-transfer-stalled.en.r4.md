This guide covers **Diagnose a stalled Vault transfer** in Dosya. The main action is to keep the app open, check connectivity and storage, then retry the individual item; the decisive check is whether the transfer resumes or reports a specific device or server error.

## Before you start

- Open Dosya Vault in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify encrypted transfer that no longer progresses.

## Steps: diagnose a stalled Vault transfer

1. Open Dosya Vault and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate encrypted transfer that no longer progresses and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Keep the app open, check connectivity and storage, then retry the individual item.
4. Confirm that the transfer resumes or reports a specific device or server error.

**Safety note.** Support cannot reconstruct a lost passphrase or recovery key; test recovery before an emergency.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The transfer resumes or reports a specific device or server error.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the transfer resumes or reports a specific device or server error.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Dosya Vault, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
