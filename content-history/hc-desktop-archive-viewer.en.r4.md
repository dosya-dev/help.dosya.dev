This guide covers **Browse an archive in the desktop app** in Dosya. The main action is to open its archive viewer and expand the required folders; the decisive check is whether entries can be inspected without extracting the whole archive.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya desktop app and locate the supported archive file.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify supported archive file.

## Steps: browse an archive in the desktop app

1. Confirm the account and workspace in the Dosya desktop app when reviewing the supported archive file.
2. Locate supported archive file and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open its archive viewer and expand the required folders.
4. Confirm that entries can be inspected without extracting the whole archive.

**Safety note.** Open untrusted archives or documents only after checking their source and filename.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Entries can be inspected without extracting the whole archive.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and entries can be inspected without extracting the whole archive.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the supported archive file, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
