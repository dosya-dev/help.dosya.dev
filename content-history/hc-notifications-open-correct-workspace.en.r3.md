This guide covers **Open a notification in the correct workspace** in Dosya. The main action is to read the workspace context, open the notification, and confirm the active workspace before acting; the decisive check is whether the link resolves to the intended resource and workspace.

## Before you start

- Open Profile > Notifications and the notification inbox in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify notification linked to a workspace resource.

## Steps: open a notification in the correct workspace

1. Open Profile > Notifications and the notification inbox and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate notification linked to a workspace resource and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Read the workspace context, open the notification, and confirm the active workspace before acting.
4. Confirm that the link resolves to the intended resource and workspace.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The link resolves to the intended resource and workspace.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the link resolves to the intended resource and workspace.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Profile > Notifications and the notification inbox, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
