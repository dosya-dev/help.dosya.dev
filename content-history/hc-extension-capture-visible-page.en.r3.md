This guide covers **Capture the visible page with the extension** in Dosya. The main action is to arrange the visible area, remove private overlays, choose screenshot capture, and save; the decisive check is whether dosya stores a screenshot of the reviewed visible content.

Keep the browser profile, signed-in account, site permission, destination workspace, and folder in view while you work.

## Before you start

- Open the Save to Dosya browser extension in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify browser tab and screenshot capture option.

## Steps: capture the visible page with the extension

1. Open the Save to Dosya browser extension and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate browser tab and screenshot capture option and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Arrange the visible area, remove private overlays, choose screenshot capture, and save.
4. Confirm that Dosya stores a screenshot of the reviewed visible content.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Dosya stores a screenshot of the reviewed visible content.

Open the saved result in Dosya and compare it with the selected page, screenshot, or file. Check the active browser profile, site permission, extension session, destination access, and the visible extension error.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and Dosya stores a screenshot of the reviewed visible content.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Save to Dosya browser extension, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
