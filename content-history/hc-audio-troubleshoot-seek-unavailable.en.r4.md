This guide covers **Handle unavailable audio seeking** in Dosya. The main action is to allow processing to complete, refresh the current version, and compare behavior with a short known-good file; the decisive check is whether seeking becomes available or the file or processing limit is identified.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya audio player in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify audio track whose waveform or seek control is unavailable.

## Steps: handle unavailable audio seeking

1. Open the Dosya audio player and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate audio track whose waveform or seek control is unavailable and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Allow processing to complete, refresh the current version, and compare behavior with a short known-good file.
4. Confirm that seeking becomes available or the file or processing limit is identified.

**Safety note.** Use one known playable audio file to separate source, network, metadata, and queue problems.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Seeking becomes available or the file or processing limit is identified.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and seeking becomes available or the file or processing limit is identified.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya audio player, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
