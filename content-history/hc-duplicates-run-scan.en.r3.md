## Before you start

- Open Duplicates in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Duplicates page for the active workspace.

## Steps: run an exact duplicate scan

1. Open Duplicates and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate Duplicates page for the active workspace and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Leave the page open while comparison progresses and refreshes its results.
4. Confirm that exact-content duplicate groups appear as they are found.
