## Before you start

- Open sharing-link Analytics and Activity in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify named-recipient share with verified access.

## Steps: match a recipient to sharing activity

1. Open sharing-link Analytics and Activity and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate named-recipient share with verified access and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Compare recipient identity with access and download events.
4. Confirm that activity is attributed only where verification data is available.
