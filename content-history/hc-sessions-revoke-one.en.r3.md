This guide covers **Revoke one unfamiliar Dosya session** in Dosya. The main action is to revoke that session and keep the current trusted session active; the decisive check is whether the selected device must sign in again.

## Before you start

- Open Profile > Security and Sessions in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify single session you do not recognize.

## Steps: revoke one unfamiliar Dosya session

1. Open Profile > Security and Sessions and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate single session you do not recognize and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Revoke that session and keep the current trusted session active.
4. Confirm that the selected device must sign in again.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The selected device must sign in again.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the selected device must sign in again.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Profile > Security and Sessions, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
