Archive preview can reveal contained paths without opening every entry. Treat unfamiliar archives cautiously.

Preview the entry list before extraction, especially when an archive came from someone else. Unexpected paths, extensions or unusually large entries deserve review.

## Steps: inspect an archive before extracting files

1. Open the archive from Files.
2. Inspect its entry list and folder paths.
3. Check for unexpected names or sizes.
4. Choose a controlled extraction destination when supported.
5. Wait for extraction to complete.
6. Verify resulting files before sharing or deleting the archive.

## Verify the result

Compare the extracted names and folder structure with the preview. Keep the source archive until the destination files open correctly.
