This guide covers **Play a video in the mobile viewer** in Dosya. The main action is to start playback, seek once, and rotate or enter fullscreen if needed; the decisive check is whether video and audio continue from the selected position.

## Before you start

- Open a media file in the Dosya mobile viewer in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify supported video file.

## Steps: play a video in the mobile viewer

1. Open a media file in the Dosya mobile viewer and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate supported video file and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Start playback, seek once, and rotate or enter fullscreen if needed.
4. Confirm that video and audio continue from the selected position.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Video and audio continue from the selected position.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and video and audio continue from the selected position.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to a media file in the Dosya mobile viewer, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
