## Before you start

- Open the Dosya audio player in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify track with blank or incorrect title, artist, or album.

## Steps: fix missing audio metadata

1. Open the Dosya audio player and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate track with blank or incorrect title, artist, or album and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Confirm the selected file and version, inspect source tags in a trusted local tool, and upload a corrected version if needed.
4. Confirm that the player shows metadata from the intended current file.

**Safety note.** Use one known playable audio file to separate source, network, metadata, and queue problems.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The player shows metadata from the intended current file.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the player shows metadata from the intended current file.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya audio player, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
