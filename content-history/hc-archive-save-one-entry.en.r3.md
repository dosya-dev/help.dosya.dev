## Before you start

- Open the Dosya archive viewer in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify reviewed archive entry and destination folder.

## Steps: save one archive entry to Dosya

1. Open the Dosya archive viewer and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate reviewed archive entry and destination folder and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose Save to Dosya for that entry, confirm the destination breadcrumb, and verify the resulting file details.
4. Confirm that one independent file is created in the intended Dosya folder.
