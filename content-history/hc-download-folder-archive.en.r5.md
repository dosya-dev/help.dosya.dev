Use this workflow to download a folder as an archive in the intended account or workspace.

Folder download prepares a local archive of the selected tree. Large folders may require more preparation time and local disk space.

## Before you start

- Open Files → Download in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify a small test folder.

## Steps: download a folder as an archive

1. Open Files → Download and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select a small test folder and check its name or identifying details.
3. Choose Download and wait for archive preparation.
4. Verify that one archive contains the expected folder tree.

**Safety note.** Keep the browser download visible until archive preparation and transfer complete.

## Verify the result

Open the archive and compare top-level name, nested paths and sample file sizes with the Dosya folder before deleting or sharing anything.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and one archive contains the expected folder tree.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Files → Download, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
