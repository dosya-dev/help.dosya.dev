This guide covers **Check access before moving into a restricted folder** in Dosya. The main action is to review your role and the destination access rules before attempting the move; the decisive check is whether the move is allowed by policy or the blocking restriction is clear.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya file list and destination picker in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify destination with member or folder restrictions.

## Steps: check access before moving into a restricted folder

1. Open the Dosya file list and destination picker and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate destination with member or folder restrictions and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Review your role and the destination access rules before attempting the move.
4. Confirm that the move is allowed by policy or the blocking restriction is clear.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The move is allowed by policy or the blocking restriction is clear.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the move is allowed by policy or the blocking restriction is clear.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya file list and destination picker, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
