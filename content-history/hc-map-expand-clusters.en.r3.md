This guide covers **Zoom and expand photo map clusters** in Dosya. The main action is to zoom in or select the cluster until individual locations separate; the decisive check is whether individual photo markers become available for inspection.

## Before you start

- Open Map in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify cluster containing more than one mapped file.

## Steps: zoom and expand photo map clusters

1. Open Map and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate cluster containing more than one mapped file and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Zoom in or select the cluster until individual locations separate.
4. Confirm that individual photo markers become available for inspection.

## Troubleshooting
