This guide covers **Troubleshoot an empty notification inbox** in Dosya. The main action is to refresh once, confirm the account and workspace, then compare notification preferences and event eligibility; the decisive check is whether the inbox either loads its history or the missing-event cause is isolated.

## Before you start

- Open Notifications in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify empty or unexpectedly short Notifications page.

## Steps: troubleshoot an empty notification inbox

1. Open Notifications and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate empty or unexpectedly short Notifications page and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Refresh once, confirm the account and workspace, then compare notification preferences and event eligibility.
4. Confirm that the inbox either loads its history or the missing-event cause is isolated.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The inbox either loads its history or the missing-event cause is isolated.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the inbox either loads its history or the missing-event cause is isolated.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Notifications, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
