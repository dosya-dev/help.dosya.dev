This guide covers **Understand always-on Dosya notifications** in Dosya. The main action is to read its note and review which safety or service events cannot be disabled; the decisive check is whether critical events remain enabled while optional events follow your preference.

## Before you start

- Open Profile > Notifications in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify group marked always on or partly always on.

## Steps: understand always-on Dosya notifications

1. Open Profile > Notifications and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate group marked always on or partly always on and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Read its note and review which safety or service events cannot be disabled.
4. Confirm that critical events remain enabled while optional events follow your preference.

**Safety note.** Some security and service messages are always on even when optional groups are disabled.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Critical events remain enabled while optional events follow your preference.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and critical events remain enabled while optional events follow your preference.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Profile > Notifications, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
