This guide covers **Review a desktop first-sync plan** in Dosya. The main action is to inspect planned uploads, downloads, conflicts, and exclusions before letting the full transfer run; the decisive check is whether the initial operations match the selected folders and mode.

## Before you start

- Open Sync settings in the Dosya desktop app in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify first-sync preview or initial activity list.

## Steps: review a desktop first-sync plan

1. Open Sync settings in the Dosya desktop app and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate first-sync preview or initial activity list and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Inspect planned uploads, downloads, conflicts, and exclusions before letting the full transfer run.
4. Confirm that the initial operations match the selected folders and mode.

**Safety note.** Plan the first sync with a small folder and enough local and workspace storage.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The initial operations match the selected folders and mode.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the initial operations match the selected folders and mode.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Sync settings in the Dosya desktop app, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
