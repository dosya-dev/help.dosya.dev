## Before you start

- Open Vault in the Dosya desktop app in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify unlocked writable Vault folder.

## Steps: upload an encrypted Vault file on desktop

1. Open Vault in the Dosya desktop app and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate unlocked writable Vault folder and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose a small test file and upload it through Vault.
4. Confirm that the encrypted item appears after transfer completion.

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The encrypted item appears after transfer completion.

## Expected result

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Vault in the Dosya desktop app, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
