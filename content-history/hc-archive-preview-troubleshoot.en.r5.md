Check the workspace, folder path and identity of an archive that shows an error. Test an archive that shows an error with disposable data and compare source and destination after refreshing.

## Before you start

- Open Archive preview in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify an archive that shows an error.

## Steps: troubleshoot an archive that will not preview

1. Open Archive preview and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select an archive that shows an error and check its name or identifying details.
3. Check format and size, then retry with a small known-good archive.
4. Verify that the comparison separates an archive problem from a preview problem.

**Safety note.** Treat archive entries as untrusted until their names and paths are reviewed.

Verify that the comparison separates an archive problem from a preview problem.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and the comparison separates an archive problem from a preview problem.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Archive preview, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
