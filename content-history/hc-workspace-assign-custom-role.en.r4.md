This guide covers **Assign a custom role to a workspace member** in Dosya. The main action is to change the member role, confirm, and test their navigation in a separate session; the decisive check is whether the member receives the custom permission set.

## Before you start

- Open Workspace settings > Roles in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify member row and newly created role.

## Steps: assign a custom role to a workspace member

1. Open Workspace settings > Roles and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate member row and newly created role and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Change the member role, confirm, and test their navigation in a separate session.
4. Confirm that the member receives the custom permission set.

**Safety note.** Grant the smallest permission set and test it with a non-administrator account.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The member receives the custom permission set.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the member receives the custom permission set.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Workspace settings > Roles, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
