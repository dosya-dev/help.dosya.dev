This guide covers **Recover desktop sync when local disk space is low** in Dosya. The main action is to free trusted local space or reduce the selected sync scope, then resume one test transfer; the decisive check is whether the app can create its local file and temporary data.

## Before you start

- Open Issues and Activity in the Dosya desktop app in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify download or two-way sync paused for device storage.

## Steps: recover desktop sync when local disk space is low

1. Open Issues and Activity in the Dosya desktop app and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate download or two-way sync paused for device storage and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Free trusted local space or reduce the selected sync scope, then resume one test transfer.
4. Confirm that the app can create its local file and temporary data.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The app can create its local file and temporary data.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the app can create its local file and temporary data.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Issues and Activity in the Dosya desktop app, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
