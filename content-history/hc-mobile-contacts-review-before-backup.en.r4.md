This guide covers **Review contacts before mobile backup** in Dosya. The main action is to inspect representative names, phone numbers, and source account before confirming; the decisive check is whether the backup selection contains the intended contacts.

## Before you start

- Open Contacts backup in the Dosya mobile app in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify prepared contact list and count.

## Steps: review contacts before mobile backup

1. Open Contacts backup in the Dosya mobile app and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate prepared contact list and count and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Inspect representative names, phone numbers, and source account before confirming.
4. Confirm that the backup selection contains the intended contacts.

**Safety note.** Use a small test contact set and confirm the device contact account before restore.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The backup selection contains the intended contacts.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the backup selection contains the intended contacts.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Contacts backup in the Dosya mobile app, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
