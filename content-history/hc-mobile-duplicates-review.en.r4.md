## Before you start

- Open the Dosya mobile app and locate the Duplicates screen in the active workspace.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Duplicates screen in the active workspace.

## Steps: review exact duplicates on mobile

1. Confirm the account and workspace in the Dosya mobile app when reviewing the Duplicates screen in the active workspace.
2. Locate Duplicates screen in the active workspace and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open a group and compare each copy before making a selection.
4. Confirm that the intended keeper and removable copies are clear.
