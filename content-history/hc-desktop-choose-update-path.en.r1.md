Choose the correct desktop update path is easier to verify when you use one controlled item and confirm the account, workspace, source, and destination at each step. This guide follows the current Dosya workflow and identifies the result you should see.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya desktop app and its settings in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify desktop installation from a known store or direct installer.
- Use a small non-confidential test item and record its current state before changing it.
- Keep passwords, API keys, recovery codes, private links, personal data, and confidential file contents out of screenshots and support messages.

## Steps: choose the correct desktop update path

1. Open the Dosya desktop app and its settings and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate desktop installation from a known store or direct installer and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Identify the installation source, use its supported updater, and verify the publisher before applying the update.
4. Read the displayed warning, permission, progress, or confirmation state before leaving the screen.
5. Confirm that the app updates through the channel that owns the installation.
6. Refresh, fetch, or reopen the relevant view once to verify the result persisted.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the app updates through the channel that owns the installation. The result remains visible after a refresh, reconnect, or return to the relevant screen.

## Limits and important details

- Available controls depend on the current client version, account plan, workspace role, policy, and selected integration surface.
- Processing, indexing, background transfer, provider authorization, or storage accounting can delay a visible result.
- For destructive, access-changing, or automated actions, validate with disposable data before applying the workflow to important files.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya desktop app and its settings, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.

**The operation is rejected or stalls.** Read the exact message and check permissions, locks, storage, network state, source availability, and any third-party authorization before retrying.

**The result is still unclear.** Repeat with one small test item, then contact support with the time, client version, workspace, and a nonsecret error, delivery, or request identifier.
