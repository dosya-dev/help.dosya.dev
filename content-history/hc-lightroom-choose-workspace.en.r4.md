This guide covers **Choose a workspace for Lightroom publishing** in Dosya. The main action is to select the workspace that should receive galleries and approve the connection; the decisive check is whether the catalog is connected to the intended workspace.

## Before you start

- Open Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic and locate the workspace selector on the Lightroom approval page.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify workspace selector on the Lightroom approval page.

## Steps: choose a workspace for Lightroom publishing

1. Confirm the account and workspace in Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic when reviewing the workspace selector on the Lightroom approval page.
2. Locate workspace selector on the Lightroom approval page and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Select the workspace that should receive galleries and approve the connection.
4. Confirm that the catalog is connected to the intended workspace.

**Safety note.** Use Lightroom Classic 10 or newer and keep the plug-in folder in a permanent local location.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The catalog is connected to the intended workspace.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the catalog is connected to the intended workspace.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the workspace selector on the Lightroom approval page, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
