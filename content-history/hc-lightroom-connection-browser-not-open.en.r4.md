## Before you start

- Open Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic and locate the Connect control and displayed approval URL.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Connect control and displayed approval URL.

## Steps: fix a Lightroom connection page that does not open

1. Confirm the account and workspace in Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic when reviewing the Connect control and displayed approval URL.
2. Locate Connect control and displayed approval URL and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Copy the displayed URL into a current browser, sign in, and approve the matching code.
4. Confirm that Lightroom receives the approval and stores the connection.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Lightroom receives the approval and stores the connection.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and Lightroom receives the approval and stores the connection.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the Connect control and displayed approval URL, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
