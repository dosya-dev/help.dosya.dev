Use this workflow to use video preview controls in the intended workspace.

Preview controls operate on browser playback and do not edit the source video. Codec support and available controls depend on the client.

## Before you start

- Open Files → Preview in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify a supported test video.

## Steps: use video preview controls

1. Open Files → Preview and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select a supported test video and check its name or identifying details.
3. Play, pause, seek and adjust volume.
4. Verify that the controls respond without downloading a separate copy.

**Safety note.** Use non-confidential test media and confirm the selected filename before playback.

## Verify the result

Seek to two points in the video and confirm playback resumes. Download the original if the preview cannot decode it reliably.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and the controls respond without downloading a separate copy.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Files → Preview, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
