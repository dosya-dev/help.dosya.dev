This guide covers **Open the mobile notification inbox** in Dosya. The main action is to open the inbox and review the newest grouped items; the decisive check is whether notifications for the signed-in account appear newest first.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya mobile app and locate the notification bell in the mobile app.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify notification bell in the mobile app.

## Steps: open the mobile notification inbox

1. Confirm the account and workspace in the Dosya mobile app when reviewing the notification bell in the mobile app.
2. Locate notification bell in the mobile app and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open the inbox and review the newest grouped items.
4. Confirm that notifications for the signed-in account appear newest first.

**Safety note.** Keep the mobile app updated and allow notifications at both the app and operating-system level.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Notifications for the signed-in account appear newest first.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and notifications for the signed-in account appear newest first.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the notification bell in the mobile app, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
