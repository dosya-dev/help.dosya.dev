This guide covers **Choose which event groups notify you** in Dosya. The main action is to enable the groups you need and adjust available individual events; the decisive check is whether optional notifications follow the saved group choices.

## Before you start

- Open Profile > Notifications in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify event-group switches and any nested event options.

## Steps: choose which event groups notify you

1. Open Profile > Notifications and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate event-group switches and any nested event options and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Enable the groups you need and adjust available individual events.
4. Confirm that optional notifications follow the saved group choices.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Optional notifications follow the saved group choices.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and optional notifications follow the saved group choices.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Profile > Notifications, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
