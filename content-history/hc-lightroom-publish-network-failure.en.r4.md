This guide covers **Troubleshoot Lightroom publish network failures** in Dosya. The main action is to restore connectivity and publish the remaining photos again without removing completed items; the decisive check is whether completed photos stay published and pending photos continue.

## Before you start

- Open Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic and locate the photos left under New or Modified Photos to Publish.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify photos left under New or Modified Photos to Publish.

## Steps: troubleshoot Lightroom publish network failures

1. Confirm the account and workspace in Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic when reviewing the photos left under New or Modified Photos to Publish.
2. Locate photos left under New or Modified Photos to Publish and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Restore connectivity and publish the remaining photos again without removing completed items.
4. Confirm that completed photos stay published and pending photos continue.

**Safety note.** Keep Lightroom open, preserve the exact error, and test with one photo before retrying a large publish.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Completed photos stay published and pending photos continue.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and completed photos stay published and pending photos continue.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the photos left under New or Modified Photos to Publish, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
