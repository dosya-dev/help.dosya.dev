Use this workflow to clear a multi-file selection in the intended workspace.

Clearing selection mode prevents a later batch action from including stale items. It does not delete, move or otherwise change the selected files.

## Before you start

- Open Files and locate an active multi-selection.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify an active multi-selection.

## Steps: clear a multi-file selection

1. Confirm the workspace in Files when reviewing an active multi-selection.
2. Select an active multi-selection and check its name or identifying details.
3. Use Clear or exit selection mode before starting another action.
4. Verify that no stale selection remains active.

**Safety note.** Review the highlighted items and selection count before applying a batch action.

## Verify the result

Confirm that the selection count disappears and no cards or rows remain highlighted before starting the next action.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and no stale selection remains active.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for an active multi-selection, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
