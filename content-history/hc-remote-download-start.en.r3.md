This guide covers **Start a remote download from a direct URL** in Dosya. The main action is to paste the link and choose Fetch file; the decisive check is whether a queued download job appears.

## Before you start

- Open Integrations > Remote Downloads in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify direct HTTPS link to a non-confidential test file.

## Steps: start a remote download from a direct URL

1. Open Integrations > Remote Downloads and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate direct HTTPS link to a non-confidential test file and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Paste the link and choose Fetch file.
4. Confirm that a queued download job appears.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: A queued download job appears.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and a queued download job appears.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Integrations > Remote Downloads, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
