This guide covers **Send an updated sharing link after changing settings** in Dosya. The main action is to save the settings, retest the same link, and tell recipients which access details changed; the decisive check is whether recipients use the current controls without receiving a conflicting duplicate share.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya sharing-link management view in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify share whose password, expiry, or recipient rules changed.

## Steps: send an updated sharing link after changing settings

1. Open the Dosya sharing-link management view and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate share whose password, expiry, or recipient rules changed and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Save the settings, retest the same link, and tell recipients which access details changed.
4. Confirm that recipients use the current controls without receiving a conflicting duplicate share.

**Safety note.** Test the final recipient experience before sending sensitive or time-limited content.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Recipients use the current controls without receiving a conflicting duplicate share.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and recipients use the current controls without receiving a conflicting duplicate share.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya sharing-link management view, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
