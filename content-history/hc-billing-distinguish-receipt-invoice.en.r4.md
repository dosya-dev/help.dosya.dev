This guide covers **Distinguish a receipt from an invoice** in Dosya. The main action is to compare the document labels, payment state, date, tax details, and transaction reference before downloading; the decisive check is whether the selected document serves the intended accounting purpose.

## Before you start

- Open Account settings > Billing in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify billing document list for a completed payment.

## Steps: distinguish a receipt from an invoice

1. Open Account settings > Billing and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate billing document list for a completed payment and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Compare the document labels, payment state, date, tax details, and transaction reference before downloading.
4. Confirm that the selected document serves the intended accounting purpose.

**Safety note.** Match the account, billing period, amount, and plan before using a document for records.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The selected document serves the intended accounting purpose.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the selected document serves the intended accounting purpose.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Account settings > Billing, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
