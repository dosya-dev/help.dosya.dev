This guide covers **Open the workspace activity log** in Dosya. The main action is to open Activity and confirm the workspace name and total event count; the decisive check is whether the newest permitted workspace events appear.

## Before you start

- Open Activity log in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Activity entry in the workspace navigation.

## Steps: open the workspace activity log

1. Open Activity log and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate Activity entry in the workspace navigation and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open Activity and confirm the workspace name and total event count.
4. Confirm that the newest permitted workspace events appear.

**Safety note.** You need the workspace activity permission; use the time and resource name to distinguish similar events.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The newest permitted workspace events appear.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the newest permitted workspace events appear.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Activity log, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
