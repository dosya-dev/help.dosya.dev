This guide covers **Resolve an OAuth and password identity mismatch** in Dosya. The main action is to return to sign-in, choose the method originally used for that email, and verify the profile after access; the decisive check is whether the session opens the expected Dosya identity and workspaces.

## Before you start

- Open Dosya sign-in and account recovery in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify account that may use Google, Apple, or password sign-in.

## Steps: resolve an OAuth and password identity mismatch

1. Open Dosya sign-in and account recovery and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate account that may use Google, Apple, or password sign-in and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Return to sign-in, choose the method originally used for that email, and verify the profile after access.
4. Confirm that the session opens the expected Dosya identity and workspaces.

**Safety note.** Use the newest recovery message and verify the account identity before setting a password.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The session opens the expected Dosya identity and workspaces.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the session opens the expected Dosya identity and workspaces.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Dosya sign-in and account recovery, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
