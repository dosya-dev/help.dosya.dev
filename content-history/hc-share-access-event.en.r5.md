Use this workflow to inspect details for a share access event in the intended account or workspace.

An access event records available time, action and device context for a particular link. It is activity evidence, not absolute proof of identity.

## Before you start

- Open Share analytics in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify one controlled recipient visit.

## Steps: inspect details for a share access event

1. Open Share analytics and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select one controlled recipient visit and check its name or identifying details.
3. Open the event and review time, action and device details.
4. Verify that the event corresponds to the controlled visit.

**Safety note.** Use a controlled recipient and non-confidential files when testing delivery settings.

## Verify the result

Match the event time and action with a controlled visit, then compare it with the link’s recipient and download settings.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and the event corresponds to the controlled visit.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Share analytics, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
