This guide covers **Choose notification delivery channels** in Dosya. The main action is to enable only the channels you can monitor and let each change save; the decisive check is whether the chosen channel switches remain set after reopening the profile.

## Before you start

- Open Profile > Notifications in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify In-app, Email, and Browser push channel switches.

## Steps: choose notification delivery channels

1. Open Profile > Notifications and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate In-app, Email, and Browser push channel switches and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Enable only the channels you can monitor and let each change save.
4. Confirm that the chosen channel switches remain set after reopening the profile.

**Safety note.** Some security and service messages are always on even when optional groups are disabled.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The chosen channel switches remain set after reopening the profile.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the chosen channel switches remain set after reopening the profile.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Profile > Notifications, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
