This guide covers **Verify access removal for a former member** in Dosya. The main action is to refresh the former member session and try to reopen a known workspace resource; the decisive check is whether the former member can no longer enter the workspace.

## Before you start

- Open Workspace settings > Team in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify removed test member and their existing session.

## Steps: verify access removal for a former member

1. Open Workspace settings > Team and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate removed test member and their existing session and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Refresh the former member session and try to reopen a known workspace resource.
4. Confirm that the former member can no longer enter the workspace.

**Safety note.** Use a separate test member to verify access after every role or membership change.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The former member can no longer enter the workspace.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the former member can no longer enter the workspace.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Workspace settings > Team, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
