## Steps: check image dimensions in Dosya

1. Open the Dosya media viewer and File details and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate known image file and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open its preview and details, compare width, height, format, and byte size with the source record.
4. Confirm that the displayed dimensions identify the intended image asset.
