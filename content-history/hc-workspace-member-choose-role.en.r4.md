This guide covers **Choose a workspace role before inviting someone** in Dosya. The main action is to compare the person responsibilities with built-in or custom role permissions and select the smallest suitable role; the decisive check is whether the invitation grants only the required starting access.

## Before you start

- Open Workspace settings > Team in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify new member and the available role list.

## Steps: choose a workspace role before inviting someone

1. Open Workspace settings > Team and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate new member and the available role list and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Compare the person responsibilities with built-in or custom role permissions and select the smallest suitable role.
4. Confirm that the invitation grants only the required starting access.

**Safety note.** Use a separate test member to verify access after every role or membership change.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The invitation grants only the required starting access.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the invitation grants only the required starting access.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Workspace settings > Team, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
