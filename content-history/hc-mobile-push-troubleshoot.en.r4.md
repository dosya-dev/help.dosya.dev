This guide covers **Fix mobile push notifications that do not arrive** in Dosya. The main action is to enable the required Dosya groups, push channel, and OS permission, then generate one controlled event; the decisive check is whether the controlled notification reaches the intended mobile device.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya mobile app and locate the mobile notification settings and device app permissions.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify mobile notification settings and device app permissions.

## Steps: fix mobile push notifications that do not arrive

1. Confirm the account and workspace in the Dosya mobile app when reviewing the mobile notification settings and device app permissions.
2. Locate mobile notification settings and device app permissions and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Enable the required Dosya groups, push channel, and OS permission, then generate one controlled event.
4. Confirm that the controlled notification reaches the intended mobile device.

**Safety note.** Keep the mobile app updated and allow notifications at both the app and operating-system level.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The controlled notification reaches the intended mobile device.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the controlled notification reaches the intended mobile device.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the mobile notification settings and device app permissions, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
