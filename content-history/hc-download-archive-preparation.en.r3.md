This guide covers **Wait for a download archive to be prepared** in Dosya. The main action is to keep the page available, wait for preparation to finish, and start the download only from the completed state; the decisive check is whether one prepared archive begins downloading.

Keep the source file, destination folder, available storage, and transfer state in view while you work.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya file list and download controls in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify folder or selection archive preparation state.

## Steps: wait for a download archive to be prepared

1. Open the Dosya file list and download controls and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate folder or selection archive preparation state and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Keep the page available, wait for preparation to finish, and start the download only from the completed state.
4. Confirm that one prepared archive begins downloading.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: One prepared archive begins downloading.

Compare the uploaded item’s name, size, and destination with the source before removing any local copy. Record the failed item and its exact transfer message before retrying; repeated blind retries can hide a size, quota, or destination problem.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and one prepared archive begins downloading.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya file list and download controls, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
