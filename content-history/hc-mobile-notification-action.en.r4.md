This guide covers **Open an item from a mobile notification** in Dosya. The main action is to select it and let Dosya route to the related mobile screen; the decisive check is whether the referenced resource or settings screen opens.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya mobile app and locate the notification with an available destination.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify notification with an available destination.

## Steps: open an item from a mobile notification

1. Confirm the account and workspace in the Dosya mobile app when reviewing the notification with an available destination.
2. Locate notification with an available destination and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Select it and let Dosya route to the related mobile screen.
4. Confirm that the referenced resource or settings screen opens.

**Safety note.** Keep the mobile app updated and allow notifications at both the app and operating-system level.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The referenced resource or settings screen opens.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the referenced resource or settings screen opens.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the notification with an available destination, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
