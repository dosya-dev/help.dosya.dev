This guide covers **Preview a supported archive entry** in Dosya. The main action is to select the exact entry, verify its path and size, and open its preview without extracting the whole archive; the decisive check is whether the viewer displays the intended entry while the stored archive remains unchanged.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya archive viewer in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify small supported file inside a trusted archive.

## Steps: preview a supported archive entry

1. Open the Dosya archive viewer and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate small supported file inside a trusted archive and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Select the exact entry, verify its path and size, and open its preview without extracting the whole archive.
4. Confirm that the viewer displays the intended entry while the stored archive remains unchanged.

**Safety note.** Inspect archive entry names and paths before previewing, saving, or downloading content.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The viewer displays the intended entry while the stored archive remains unchanged.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the viewer displays the intended entry while the stored archive remains unchanged.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya archive viewer, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
