This guide covers **Recover a file-request invitation that was not received** in Dosya. The main action is to choose Resend and verify the updated sent status; the decisive check is whether the recipient receives another valid invitation without a duplicate request.

## Before you start

- Open a Dosya file request in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify recipient who has not completed the request.

## Steps: resend a file-request invitation

1. Open a Dosya file request and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate recipient who has not completed the request and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose Resend and verify the updated sent status.
4. Confirm that the recipient receives another valid invitation without a duplicate request.

**Safety note.** Use a test recipient and verify the destination before changing a live request.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The recipient receives another valid invitation without a duplicate request.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the recipient receives another valid invitation without a duplicate request.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to a Dosya file request, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
