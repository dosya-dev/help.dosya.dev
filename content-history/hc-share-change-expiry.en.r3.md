This guide covers **Extend or shorten a sharing-link expiry** in Dosya. The main action is to choose the new expiry, save, and verify the displayed deadline; the decisive check is whether the link follows the updated expiry.

## Before you start

- Open a Dosya sharing link in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify link with a current expiry date.

## Steps: extend or shorten a sharing-link expiry

1. Open a Dosya sharing link and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate link with a current expiry date and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose the new expiry, save, and verify the displayed deadline.
4. Confirm that the link follows the updated expiry.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The link follows the updated expiry.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the link follows the updated expiry.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to a Dosya sharing link, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
