This guide covers **Open Dosya from the system tray or menu bar** in Dosya. The main action is to open its menu and choose the command that shows the main window; the decisive check is whether the existing desktop window becomes visible and focused.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya desktop app and locate the Dosya tray or menu-bar icon.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Dosya tray or menu-bar icon.

## Steps: open Dosya from the system tray or menu bar

1. Confirm the account and workspace in the Dosya desktop app when reviewing the Dosya tray or menu-bar icon.
2. Locate Dosya tray or menu-bar icon and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open its menu and choose the command that shows the main window.
4. Confirm that the existing desktop window becomes visible and focused.

**Safety note.** Keyboard commands follow the focused window and may differ by operating system.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The existing desktop window becomes visible and focused.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the existing desktop window becomes visible and focused.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the Dosya tray or menu-bar icon, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
