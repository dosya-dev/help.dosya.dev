Useful support reports are specific and safe. Include identifiers that support asks for through the private form, but never authentication secrets.

A useful support report explains the task, time, client, expected result and exact error without exposing credentials or confidential content. Redact screenshots before attaching them.

## Steps: share diagnostic information with support safely

1. Check service status first.
2. Write the task, expected result and actual result.
3. Include approximate time, client and active workspace name when safe.
4. Copy the exact error and redact tokens or private URLs.
5. Attach only non-confidential screenshots with sensitive areas removed.
6. Submit through private support and keep the ticket reference.

## Verify the result

Review every field and attachment before submission. Confirm passwords, keys, recovery codes, private links, payment details and private file contents are absent.

The share diagnostic information with support safely workflow completes for the test item and its result remains visible after a refresh or reconnect.
