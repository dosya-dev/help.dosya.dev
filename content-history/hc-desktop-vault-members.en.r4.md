This guide covers **Review Vault members in the desktop app** in Dosya. The main action is to compare members and roles, then make one controlled access change if authorized; the decisive check is whether the Space shows the intended member access.

## Before you start

- Open Vault in the Dosya desktop app in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Space member controls.

## Steps: review Vault members in the desktop app

1. Open Vault in the Dosya desktop app and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate Space member controls and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Compare members and roles, then make one controlled access change if authorized.
4. Confirm that the Space shows the intended member access.

**Safety note.** Vault encryption happens on the device; keep the recovery key outside Dosya.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The Space shows the intended member access.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the Space shows the intended member access.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Vault in the Dosya desktop app, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
