Cancellation stops renewal; it is different from account deletion. Review the displayed end date and prepare for the lower future storage allowance before confirming.

Cancellation and downgrade can take effect later than the confirmation click. Record the effective date and compare future storage allowance with current usage.

## Before you start

- Download any files you must keep locally.
- Compare used storage with the future plan limit.
- Check workspace owners and important automated uploads.
- Read the live cancellation or downgrade terms in full.

## Steps: cancel or downgrade

1. Open **Billing** and review the current plan, renewal date and usage.
2. For cancellation, select **Cancel subscription** and read the confirmation.
3. For a plan change, use **Change plan** and review the target allowance and effective date.
4. Confirm only after recording the end or change date.
5. Return to Billing and check for **Cancelling**, the end date, or the new plan.
6. Reduce usage before the lower allowance applies; use **Resume subscription** if you reverse a scheduled cancellation in time.

## Verify the result

Reload Billing and confirm the scheduled state, target plan and effective date. Reduce usage or add capacity before a lower allowance applies.

Refresh or reopen the relevant page before relying on the result. If the state differs, stop before repeating the action and preserve the exact nonsecret message for the troubleshooting checks below.

## Expected result

Billing clearly shows the scheduled state and effective date. At the lower allowance, uploads can be paused while existing files remain available under the displayed retention and access terms.

## Limits and account effects

- Cancellation is not account deletion and does not immediately remove the account.
- The live page states the applicable paid-storage end and any grace period.
- Over-limit restrictions can affect every workspace.
- A lower plan may change features as well as capacity.

## Troubleshooting

**The subscription still says active.** Check whether it also says **Cancelling** and shows an end date.

**You changed your mind.** Use **Resume subscription** while available and verify the cancelling badge disappears.

**Usage exceeds the future limit.** Download, permanently remove unneeded data, or choose sufficient storage before the date.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I confirm I used the correct account or workspace?

Check the account email, workspace name and current path before the action and again when verifying it. Similar names can refer to separate cloud-storage data and policies.

### What should I include when asking support for help?

Include the action time, client and version, expected result and exact nonsecret error. Never include passwords, API keys, recovery codes, private links, payment details or confidential file contents.
