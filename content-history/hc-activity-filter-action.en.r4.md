## Before you start

- Open Activity log in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Action filter after optionally choosing a category.

## Steps: filter activity by action

1. Open Activity log and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate Action filter after optionally choosing a category and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Select the precise action type and reset to page one.
4. Confirm that the list narrows to matching action rows.

**Safety note.** You need the workspace activity permission; use the time and resource name to distinguish similar events.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The list narrows to matching action rows.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the list narrows to matching action rows.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Activity log, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
