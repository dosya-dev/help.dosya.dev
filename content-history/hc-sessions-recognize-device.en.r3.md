This guide covers **Recognize devices in your Dosya session list** in Dosya. The main action is to compare device, browser, approximate location, and last-active time with your own usage; the decisive check is whether known sessions are distinguished from unfamiliar ones.

## Before you start

- Open Profile > Security and Sessions in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify active session row.

## Steps: recognize devices in your Dosya session list

1. Open Profile > Security and Sessions and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate active session row and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Compare device, browser, approximate location, and last-active time with your own usage.
4. Confirm that known sessions are distinguished from unfamiliar ones.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Known sessions are distinguished from unfamiliar ones.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and known sessions are distinguished from unfamiliar ones.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Profile > Security and Sessions, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
