This guide covers **Permanently delete selected test items** in Dosya. The main action is to select only the disposable entries, choose permanent deletion, read the warning, and confirm; the decisive check is whether the selected entries leave Deleted items and cannot be restored there.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya Deleted items view in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify reviewed disposable items in Deleted items.

## Steps: permanently delete selected test items

1. Open the Dosya Deleted items view and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate reviewed disposable items in Deleted items and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Select only the disposable entries, choose permanent deletion, read the warning, and confirm.
4. Confirm that the selected entries leave Deleted items and cannot be restored there.

**Safety note.** Restore first when uncertain; permanent deletion cannot be undone through Deleted items.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The selected entries leave Deleted items and cannot be restored there.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the selected entries leave Deleted items and cannot be restored there.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to the Dosya Deleted items view, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
