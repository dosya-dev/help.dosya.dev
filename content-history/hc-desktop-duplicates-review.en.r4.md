This guide covers **Review duplicate files in the desktop app** in Dosya. The main action is to compare names, paths, sizes, and dates before selecting copies; the decisive check is whether the keeper and removable duplicates are clear.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya desktop app and locate the Duplicates screen and one exact-match group.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Duplicates screen and one exact-match group.

## Steps: review duplicate files in the desktop app

1. Confirm the account and workspace in the Dosya desktop app when reviewing the Duplicates screen and one exact-match group.
2. Locate Duplicates screen and one exact-match group and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Compare names, paths, sizes, and dates before selecting copies.
4. Confirm that the keeper and removable duplicates are clear.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The keeper and removable duplicates are clear.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the keeper and removable duplicates are clear.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the Duplicates screen and one exact-match group, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
