This guide covers **Connect a Lightroom catalog to Dosya** in Dosya. The main action is to choose Connect, sign in in the opened browser, and approve the code shown by Lightroom; the decisive check is whether lightroom reports the chosen workspace as connected.

## Before you start

- Open Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic and locate the dosya.dev entry in Plug-in Manager.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify dosya.dev entry in Plug-in Manager.

## Steps: connect a Lightroom catalog to Dosya

1. Confirm the account and workspace in Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic when reviewing the dosya.dev entry in Plug-in Manager.
2. Locate dosya.dev entry in Plug-in Manager and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Choose Connect, sign in in the opened browser, and approve the code shown by Lightroom.
4. Confirm that Lightroom reports the chosen workspace as connected.

**Safety note.** Use Lightroom Classic 10 or newer and keep the plug-in folder in a permanent local location.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Lightroom reports the chosen workspace as connected.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and Lightroom reports the chosen workspace as connected.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the dosya.dev entry in Plug-in Manager, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
