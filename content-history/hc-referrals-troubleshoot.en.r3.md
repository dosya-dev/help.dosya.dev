A missing reward usually means the invitation was not attributed, the invited account has not completed the current qualification, or the account ledger has not refreshed yet.

Referral storage appears only after the invited account meets the displayed qualification rules. A later visit to the link cannot always repair an account created without it.

## Before you start

- Record the invited email only if the person consents to private support use.
- Check the current reward terms and cap.
- Do not create duplicate accounts to force qualification.
- Keep referral details out of public comments.

## Steps: trace the referral status

1. Open Referrals and locate the invited friend.
2. Read the exact Pending, Credited or other status.
3. Confirm the person originally used your full referral URL.
4. Ask them to complete the displayed verification requirement.
5. Reload Referrals and Billing once after qualification.
6. Escalate privately with the approximate invitation time and visible status if it remains wrong.

## Verify the result

Compare the visible referral status with Billing after qualification. Escalate with approximate times and status text, never private account credentials.

Refresh or reopen the relevant page before relying on the result. If the refreshed state differs, stop before repeating the action and preserve the exact nonsecret message for the troubleshooting checks below.

## Expected result

You identify the referral stage or see the credited bonus in the account storage total.

## Limits and important details

- Support cannot credit an invitation that does not meet current eligibility rules.
- Qualification and anti-abuse checks can prevent or delay credit.
- A referral bonus does not replace an active subscription automatically.
- Workspace-level storage caps remain separate.

## Troubleshooting

**No friend row appears.** The sign-up may not have used the referral URL; preserve the link and timing for private review.

**The row is pending.** Complete the exact verification or qualification shown by the current program.

**The row is credited but storage is unchanged.** Reload Billing and provide the credited status to private support.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I confirm I used the correct account or workspace?

Check the account email and workspace name before the action and again when verifying the result. Similar names in another workspace refer to separate cloud-storage data and access policies.

### What should I include when asking support for help?

Include the action time, client and version, active workspace, expected result and exact nonsecret error. Never include passwords, API keys, webhook secrets, recovery codes, payment details or confidential file contents.
