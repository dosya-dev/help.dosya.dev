This guide covers **Unlock Vault in the desktop app** in Dosya. The main action is to enter the Vault passphrase and wait for local key setup; the decisive check is whether encrypted Spaces and files become available on this device.

## Before you start

- Open Vault in the Dosya desktop app in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Vault unlock screen.

## Steps: unlock Vault in the desktop app

1. Open Vault in the Dosya desktop app and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate Vault unlock screen and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Enter the Vault passphrase and wait for local key setup.
4. Confirm that encrypted Spaces and files become available on this device.

**Safety note.** Vault encryption happens on the device; keep the recovery key outside Dosya.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Encrypted Spaces and files become available on this device.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and encrypted Spaces and files become available on this device.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Vault in the Dosya desktop app, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
