This guide covers **Report a sharing-link access problem** in Dosya. The main action is to record the time and nonsecret message, then contact the sender without forwarding passwords or verification codes; the decisive check is whether the sender has enough safe detail to review the link settings.

## Before you start

- Open a Dosya sharing-link recipient page in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify recipient error that persists with the current link.

## Steps: report a sharing-link access problem

1. Open a Dosya sharing-link recipient page and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate recipient error that persists with the current link and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Record the time and nonsecret message, then contact the sender without forwarding passwords or verification codes.
4. Confirm that the sender has enough safe detail to review the link settings.

**Safety note.** Recipients should use the exact current link and receive passwords through a separate channel.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The sender has enough safe detail to review the link settings.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the sender has enough safe detail to review the link settings.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to a Dosya sharing-link recipient page, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
