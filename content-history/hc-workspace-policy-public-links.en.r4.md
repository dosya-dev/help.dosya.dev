This guide covers **Restrict public sharing links in a workspace** in Dosya. The main action is to disable or limit public links, save, and test link creation from a non-owner member session; the decisive check is whether member sharing controls follow the workspace rule.

## Before you start

- Open Workspace settings > Policies in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify public-link policy control.

## Steps: restrict public sharing links in a workspace

1. Open Workspace settings > Policies and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate public-link policy control and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Disable or limit public links, save, and test link creation from a non-owner member session.
4. Confirm that member sharing controls follow the workspace rule.

**Safety note.** Test policy changes with a non-owner account before relying on them.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Member sharing controls follow the workspace rule.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and member sharing controls follow the workspace rule.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Workspace settings > Policies, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
