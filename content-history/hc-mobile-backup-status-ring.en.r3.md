## Before you start

- Open Dosya mobile backup in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify backup status card or ring.

## Steps: read the mobile backup status ring

1. Open Dosya mobile backup and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate backup status card or ring and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Compare completed, pending, and attention counts with the current library.
4. Confirm that the ring and counts describe the current backup state.
