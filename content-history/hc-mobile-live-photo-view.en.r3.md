## Before you start

- Open a media file in the Dosya mobile viewer in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify paired Live Photo or motion photo.

## Steps: view a Live Photo on mobile

1. Open a media file in the Dosya mobile viewer and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate paired Live Photo or motion photo and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open it and use the motion playback control.
4. Confirm that the still image and motion component play together.

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The still image and motion component play together.

## Expected result

## Troubleshooting
