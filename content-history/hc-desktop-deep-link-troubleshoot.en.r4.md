This guide covers **Troubleshoot a desktop deep link** in Dosya. The main action is to confirm the app is installed and signed in, reopen the link, and approve the OS handoff if prompted; the decisive check is whether the app opens the intended supported destination or the browser fallback remains available.

## Before you start

- Open the Dosya desktop app and locate the Dosya link expected to open the installed desktop app.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Dosya link expected to open the installed desktop app.

## Steps: troubleshoot a desktop deep link

1. Confirm the account and workspace in the Dosya desktop app when reviewing the Dosya link expected to open the installed desktop app.
2. Locate Dosya link expected to open the installed desktop app and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Confirm the app is installed and signed in, reopen the link, and approve the OS handoff if prompted.
4. Confirm that the app opens the intended supported destination or the browser fallback remains available.

**Safety note.** Keyboard commands follow the focused window and may differ by operating system.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: The app opens the intended supported destination or the browser fallback remains available.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and the app opens the intended supported destination or the browser fallback remains available.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** If the required control is missing for the Dosya link expected to open the installed desktop app, check the active workspace, your role and the client version.
