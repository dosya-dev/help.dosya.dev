Use this workflow to open the billing management portal in the intended account or workspace.

The billing portal manages the subscription and payment method for the identified billing account. Verify the account before changing external payment details.

## Before you start

- Open Billing settings in the current released Dosya client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify the workspace billing account.

## Steps: open the billing management portal

1. Open Billing settings and confirm the active workspace.
2. Select the workspace billing account and check its name or identifying details.
3. Choose Manage billing and verify the portal account.
4. Verify that the portal shows the active subscription and payment method.

## Verify the result

Return to Dosya after the portal action, refresh Billing and confirm the subscription or payment-method summary reflects the intended change.

## Expected result

The workflow completes for the controlled test and the portal shows the active subscription and payment method.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Billing settings, confirm the active workspace and check that your role permits the action.
