## Before you start

- Open Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic and locate the published collection and its remembered gallery link.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify published collection and its remembered gallery link.

## Steps: fix a Lightroom gallery that is missing online

1. Confirm the account and workspace in Lightroom Classic and Integrations > Lightroom Classic when reviewing the published collection and its remembered gallery link.
2. Locate published collection and its remembered gallery link and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Open the gallery from Plug-in Extras, then check the integration page for a removed, revoked, or expired link.
4. Confirm that the live gallery opens or the link state explains the missing page.
