This guide covers **Read notification groups by day** in Dosya. The main action is to review the group label, icon, title, message, and time for each relevant item; the decisive check is whether each notification is understood in its event-time context.

## Before you start

- Open Notifications in the current released client.
- Confirm the active account and workspace, then identify Today, Yesterday, or dated group in the inbox.

## Steps: read notification groups by day

1. Open Notifications and confirm the active account and workspace.
2. Locate Today, Yesterday, or dated group in the inbox and verify its name or identifying details.
3. Review the group label, icon, title, message, and time for each relevant item.
4. Confirm that each notification is understood in its event-time context.

**Safety note.** Confirm the signed-in account because notification history belongs to the current user.

## Verify the result

After completing the steps, check the expected result: Each notification is understood in its event-time context.

## Expected result

The controlled workflow completes and each notification is understood in its event-time context.

## Troubleshooting

**The expected control is missing.** Return to Notifications, confirm the active workspace, and check that your role and client version support the action.
